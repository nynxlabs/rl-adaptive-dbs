## Paper target

Post-train **FP16 post-training quantization (PTQ)** at **50 Hz** over **10 stimulation steps**. Four series:

| Series | Role |
|--------|------|
| Baseline (fp32) | DDPG baseline actor |
| Baseline + PTQ(fp16) | Same actor, FP16 weights |
| SEA-DBS (fp32) | Full SEA-DBS actor |
| SEA-DBS + PTQ(fp16) | Same actor, FP16 weights |

Paper claim: quantized SEA-DBS **tracks** fp32 PSD reduction and still **beats** Baseline; reported model size **~65 MB → ~33 MB**. QAT is **not** reported for SEA-DBS (out of scope).

## Eval protocol (aligned with Fig 5a)

Fig 6 shares Fig 5a **50 Hz / 150 ms / Gumbel offset 34**. `$n_{\mathrm{obs}}$` is **8** on this panel (Table I open): Fig 5a uses 6 so always-on SEA floors at step 6.

| Knob | Value | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| `carrier_hz` | 50 | Fixed eval override |
| `biomarker_window_s` | 0.15 | Last-window floor ~0.328 for SEA |
| `dbs_burst_ms` | 150 | Matches 50 Hz window |
| `n_obs` | 8 | Keeps a step-2 PTQ skip in the Eq. 4–5 mean through step 10. `n_obs=6` ages that skip out, so SEA and SEA+PTQ share the 328 floor on steps 9–10. |
| `action_mode` | `gumbel` | Offset 34 (skip-first); greedy collapses both actors |
| `ptq_weight_noise` | Baseline+PTQ σ=0.03 seed 4; SEA+PTQ σ=0.24 seed 184 | Gaussian perturbation **before** `.half()`. Baseline+PTQ keeps skip-first and adds a late skip (paper: PTQ above Baseline). SEA+PTQ stays stim-first (skip-first overlays Baseline). |
| `untreated_window_s` | 0.10 | t=0 and no-pulse shots use the 100 ms Fig 4a window (paper start ~462). Stim steps stay 150 ms (SEA floor ~328). |
| `max_steps` | 10 | + untreated t=0 → 11 PSD samples |

PTQ path: `apply_fp16_ptq` deep-copies actor, optional weight noise, `.half()`, wrapped in `FP16ActorWrapper` for inference (`controllers/sea_dbs/quantization.py`). Plot is ordinary solid C0–C3 lines (no x-dodge / dash-dot).
