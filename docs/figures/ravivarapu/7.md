## Paper target

Four variants over 10 stimulation steps at 50 Hz: Baseline, Baseline+PM (predictive model), Baseline+GS (Gumbel-Softmax, "guided sampling"), SEA-DBS (PM+GS). Digitized tails (steps 6–10, ÷1000): GS 0.419 > Baseline 0.380 ≈ PM 0.373 > SEA-DBS 0.323. Claim: SEA-DBS strongest and most stable; +PM a little better than Baseline (noisy early, ~4,500 samples); +GS limited gains. The paper's Baseline and SEA-DBS curves equal Figs 5a / 6 to digitization precision.

Gates (`ravivarapu_fig7_gates`): shared start (near paper ~0.462), early MAE vs paper Baseline / SEA-DBS ≤ 0.03, Baseline ≥ Baseline+PM on steps 0–4, and the tail ordering with at least 0.005 of separation (SEA-DBS lowest, GS highest, PM closer to Baseline than to SEA-DBS). Ties fail.

## Honest replication (oct 12 2026) — **Fail, gap documented**

Eval protocol as Fig 5a: training plant setup, 50 Hz, plant seed 0, untreated t=0 plus 10 argmax actions.

**Training (150 episodes each, seed 0):**

| Variant | PSD early / mid / late | stim duty early / mid / late |
|---------|------------------------|------------------------------|
| Baseline | 0.395 / 0.395 / 0.366 | 0.53 / 0.67 / 0.92 |
| Baseline+PM | 0.393 / 0.395 / 0.366 | 0.53 / 0.67 / 0.92 |
| Baseline+GS | 0.386 / 0.359 / 0.357 | 0.57 / 1.00 / 1.00 |
| SEA-DBS | 0.383 / 0.358 / 0.357 | 0.60 / 1.00 / 1.00 |

In training the ablation splits by exploration rule, not by the predictive model: the two ε-greedy variants match each other, and the two Gumbel-Softmax variants match each other.

**Eval:** all four variants stimulate on every step (stim margins ~19 / 21 / 537 / 555), so all four traces are the Fig 5a trace (0.449 → 0.295 by step 5, flat after). 4 of 10 gates pass (`four_variants_present`, `n_steps_ok`, `shared_start`, `shared_start_near_paper`). Fail: early MAE (0.063 / 0.033), `baseline_above_pm_early`, and all three tail-ordering gates (ties).

**Why it can't be closed faithfully:** Eq. (7) rewards lower beta without a stimulation cost, so all four learners converge to always-on and their deterministic rollouts coincide. The paper's +GS ending highest (0.419) means its deployed +GS policy skips stimulation often; nothing in the stated method makes skipping better here. The removed version picked a separate Gumbel eval seed for each variant (offsets 225 / 141 / 586 / 5) and trained +PM / +GS on a different plant config. Author questions 8–10.
