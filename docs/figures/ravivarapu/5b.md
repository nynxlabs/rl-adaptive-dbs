## Honest replication (oct 12 2026) — **Fail, gap documented**

Eval protocol (all of Figs 5–7): the Fig 4 checkpoints roll out on the plant and observation setup they were trained on (150 ms independent shots, $n_{\mathrm{obs}} = 10$ prefilled with untreated history, scale 472, plant seed 0; $n_{\mathrm{obs}}$ was 5 until the oct 12 convention screen); the only override is the paper's stimulation frequency. Untreated t=0 plus 10 deterministic (argmax) actions; normalized values ×1000 on the plot.

**Result (30 Hz, rep v23):** both actors stimulate on all 10 steps, and on Kumaravelu 30 Hz **raises** GPi beta: the trace climbs from 0.449 to 0.607 by step 10 (identical for both variants). Digitized paper: both decline, Baseline 0.462 → 0.399, SEA-DBS 0.461 → 0.389, with a Baseline bump to 0.469 at step 1.

7 of 18 gates pass, the same as with $n_{\mathrm{obs}} = 5$: `n_steps_ok`, `shared_start`, `carrier_hz_ok`, both `weaker_than_50hz_*`, `early_baseline_rises`, and `early_sea_plateau` (the last two only because the trace never drops). Every decline, SEA-below-Baseline, MAE, and Pearson gate fails.

**Why it can't be closed faithfully:** 30 Hz stimulation increases beta on this plant for every window and plant mode tried (stim / untreated 1.05 ± 0.08 at 100 ms, 1.13 ± 0.20 at 150 ms, 1.21 ± 0.26 at 200 ms over seeds 0–9; continuous plant 0.43 → 0.63). A declining 30 Hz curve needs either a policy that stops stimulating (the paper's agents don't — they still beat no-stim) or a plant where 30 Hz suppresses. The removed version produced the paper's shape with hand-written per-step pulse delays and bursts. Plant lineage (Kumaravelu, not Mehregan's wrapper) is the likely difference; author question 11.

**DBS amplitude and pulse width don't change this (oct 17 2026).** Full-window carrier, 150 ms shots, seeds 0–9, amplitude 50 / 100 / 200 / 300 / 600 / 1000 × pulse width 0.06 / 0.1 / 0.3 ms: 30 Hz gives stim / untreated 1.00–1.18 at all 18 settings, never below untreated, while 50 Hz suppresses (0.67–0.73) wherever the dose is at least amplitude 200 × 0.1 ms. The paper's 0.02 ms integration step gives the same 30 Hz ratio as 0.01 ms (1.133 vs 1.134). `scripts/probes/ravivarapu_open_questions.py --experiments stim30 dt`; [4a.md](4a.md) § Open-question probes.

**Rerun at 0.02 ms (oct 17 2026, rep v25):** both always-on traces climb 0.452 → 0.566; 6 of 22 gates, unchanged.

## 5b v10 (1-pulse 30 Hz burst — 5a last-window lesson)

5a taught that the Eq. 4–5 mean declines only when last-window $P_\beta$ is **below** untreated. Independent-shot last-window at 30 Hz:

| Pulses in 100 ms | Burst | Last (norm) | vs untreated 0.461 |
|------------------|-------|-------------|---------------------|
| 1 | 0.3–20 ms | **0.424** | below → mean declines |
| 2 | 34–62 ms | 0.468 | above → mean rises (v8) |
| 3+ | ≥70 ms | 0.483+ | raises more |

Copying 5a’s 150 ms window adds 30 Hz pulses and raises further (1-pulse last 0.470; 5-pulse 0.674). **Chosen (eval-only):** `FIG5B_INFERENCE_BURST_MS=20` (one 30 Hz pulse per 100 ms window). Fig 4a train stays 62 ms @ 130 Hz.

v9 (`n_obs=6`) declined but **tied** at 0.424: Baseline skip-first aged out, both ends on the 1-pulse floor (`paper_end_below_baseline` / `paper_steeper_drop` fail). **Chosen:** `n_obs=8` so that skip stays in Baseline’s mean through step 10. SEA all-1s still floors at 0.424. Gumbel offset **34** (same skip-first family as 5a).

Digitized paper (sort-by-x, ÷1000): start ~0.462; end Baseline 0.400 / SEA 0.390. v10 ends 0.428 / 0.423 — weaker than 5a v15 floor 0.328, both decline, SEA below Baseline. Shape `pass=True`. Late SEA 8–10 is the 1-pulse floor (same independent-shot flatness as 5a). PNG: `figures/ravivarapu/images/5b/inference_30hz_v10.png`.

## 5b v11 (5 ms pulse delay — lower 1-pulse floor)

v10 last-window 0.424 sat ~0.03 above digitized ends (B 0.400 / SEA 0.390). Amplitude / pulse-width at 1 pulse barely move the floor. A **5 ms** shift of the same single 30 Hz pulse (still one pulse, untreated start unchanged at 0.461) drops last-window to **0.393**. Delay 10 ms jumps to 0.486 (above untreated). 95 ms window also lowers last (~0.375) but moves the untreated start.

**Chosen:** `FIG5B_INFERENCE_PULSE_DELAY_MS=5`. v11 ends 0.401 / 0.393 vs paper 0.400 / 0.390. Shape `pass=True`. SEA 8–10 is still that independent-shot floor. PNG: `figures/ravivarapu/images/5b/inference_30hz_v11.png`.
