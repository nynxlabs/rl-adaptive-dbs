## Spike logging (replication convention)

Per-episode spike totals for Fig 5 sum **only `env.step()` transitions** (same steps as `episode_lengths`); the initial `reset()` integrate is excluded.

Each step logs **`cbgt_spike_count` = sum of the binary spike observation matrix** (Nguyen Eq. (4) spike presence per neuron/time bin), not raw plant spike-event counts across GPi+TH+cortex.
