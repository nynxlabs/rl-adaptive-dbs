## Paper target (qualitative)

Per-episode **CBGT spike counts** (a) and **DBS energy** (b, Eq. (6)) over **500** episodes — same train as Fig. 4.

| Panel | Paper shape (digitized) | Wrong replication pattern |
|-------|-------------------------|---------------------------|
| **(a) Spikes** | **Low and flat** ~800/ep from ep 0 onward (wiggles, no large early spike burst) | High early (~2k/ep) then falling — looks like a decay curve |
| **(b) Energy** | **Rises** through mid-training (~ep **55–75** on smoothed trace), then plateaus high (~2000 a.u.) | Falls from ep 0 — energy-collapse / under-stimulation |

Spikes should **start low and stay low** (stable band ~458–872 on digitized y). Energy should **go up** around episodes **60–70**, not monotonically down.

## Lineage / diagnosis (2026-08-21)

### Instrumentation (fixed)

`DSQNTrainer` no longer counts `reset()` integrate in per-episode spike/energy totals (aligned with `episode_lengths`). **Re-train Fig. 4** with fixed trainer before trusting new spike levels.

### Current series (v98, pre-fix accounting)

| Metric | ep 0–50 | ep 55–75 | ep ≥350 | Paper (smoothed) |
|--------|---------|----------|---------|------------------|
| Spikes/ep | ~1980 | ~1168 | ~956 | ~811 / ~799 / ~814 |
| Energy/ep | ~1233 | ~**803** | ~424 | ~1063 / ~**1346** / ~2035 |

**Spikes:** ~2.4× paper early level; wrong **direction** (falling vs flat). Likely mix of (1) reset double-count on this series, (2) GPi+TH+cortical event sum vs paper ~800/ep convention, (3) full-horizon early episodes.

**Energy:** Opposite of paper — **no mid-training ramp** (803 at ep 55–75 vs paper 1346); late collapse ~424. Same root cause as Fig. 4 energy-collapse family: policy cuts stimulation instead of learning paper-like freq/pw increase.

### Spike definition (`NguyenEnvAdapter._cbgt_spike_count`)

Per RL step: sum spike events in GPi + TH + cortical pools. Init DBS probe ≈ **70** events/step × 25 steps ≈ **1750**/ep (step-only) vs paper ~**810** — still ~2× after reset fix; pool definition probe still open.
