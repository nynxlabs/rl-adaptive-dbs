## Lineage / diagnosis (2026-07-31)

### What we know from Fig 3

Untreated **PD On** α–β (7–35 Hz, 100 ms): mean ~291, Q1 ~262. θ=150 sits **below** typical PD On mass (~0.6% of untreated windows < 150). Sustained sub-threshold control needs **DBS suppression**, not luck.

### v2 (main, Jul 27) — wrong reward sign

Distance term used **`+d`** when above θ → rewards in **positive** millions. Gates failed: late reward worse than early; lengths stuck at 25.

### v4 (paper-faithful) — sign fixed, still no early-stop

Eq. (7) distance **`−d`**; rewards ~−10⁶ → −6×10⁵. Passes scale + late>early; **fails** length drop and near-zero late reward.

| Evidence | Finding |
|----------|---------|
| Only **2 / 500** episodes length < 25 (eps 3, 31) | Early-stop almost never fires |
| Late energy ~141/ep vs early ~497/ep | Policy learns **weaker** stimulation |
| Paper end triple ~262 nA / 78.65 Hz / 1 ms | Higher energy than late v4 policy |
| Per-step $d ≈ (αβ−150)^2$ ~10⁴ when α–β~260 | Dominates τ=1 / δE=0.01·E |

**Hypothesis:** agent can shave −δE without ever reaching θ; terminal bonus (≤25·τ) is tiny vs $d$, so length never drops.

### Open question (probe first)

Can **any** fixed STN triple on Kumaravelu + `alpha_beta_power` (7–35 Hz, 100 ms) achieve **3 consecutive** steps < 150?

Probe: `scripts/probes/nguyen_fig4_alpha_beta_reachability.py` (init / paper anchor / 130 Hz / strong / min-energy).
