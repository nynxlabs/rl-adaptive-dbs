# Mehregan Fig 4a — training beta power vs step

Panel spec and **experimental tuning notes** for Mehregan et al. Fig 4a (§IV.A.1). Ship status, replication image, and run commands remain in [figures/mehregan/replications.md](../../../figures/mehregan/replications.md) § Fig 4a.

**Entry point:** `scripts/figures/papers/mehregan/4a/plot.py`

---

## Paper target

Per-step GPi beta-band power during DDPG training on the **45 Hz** fixed-mean pattern alphabet: **300** environment steps (10 episodes × 30 steps). Y-axis **PSD(x10³)** = raw $P_\beta / 1000$. Digitized paper: early (steps ≤130) mean ~0.49, late (≥150) ~0.38, drop ~0.11; mid [120,150] ~0.445 vs early [0,100] ~0.49 (modest mid fade, not a vertical cliff). Gates track **drop size, late/early ratio, and mid fade** vs that digitization (seed changes absolute level).

## Paper-faithful replication stack

Mehregan §IV.A.1 fixes the **schedule** (10×30 steps, 45 Hz init, LRs, buffer) and **discrete patterns at fixed mean rate**; it does **not** specify online exploration or irregular-pattern construction. Our documented conventions:

| Layer | Choice | Notes |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Alphabet | `FixedMeanPatternAlphabet` — 41 patterns, pattern 0 regular, 1–40 ±⅓ ISI jitter | [environment.md](../../environment.md) §4.2 |
| Plant | Python Kumaravelu, `plant.dt_ms=0.02` | §IV.A.1 |
| Observation | `state_length=1` | Paper Fig 4a scalar $P_\beta$ |
| **Alg. 1 interaction (deploy)** | greedy softmax + argmax, critic on **logits** | `fig4a_ddpg_config(exploration_mode="greedy", critic_action_input="logits")` |
| **Fig 4a training curve (panel)** | softmax τ **3→1.0**, `critic_action_input=one_hot`, `init_bias_scale=0.5` | mid-fade digitization match |

**Greedy baseline (done):** `--exploration greedy --critic-action-input logits` collapses to pattern 0 immediately (300/300 action 0). High rewards; **wrong Fig 4a shape** (episode plateaus, no cliff). Artifacts: `series_greedy_baseline.json`, `training_beta_greedy_baseline.png`. Doctrinal Algorithm 1 floor — not the panel profile.

**Not for Fig 4a panel:** tiered jitter alphabet, gradual_c-style delayed anneal / high entropy (worktree diagnostics only).

## skip_regular training (Fig 5a path — not Fig 4a)

At 45 Hz, pattern 0 (regular periodic) is the global open-loop optimum. A **41-pattern** Fig 4a agent correctly collapses to it; Mehregan Fig 5a requires trained **above** periodic 45 Hz, which needs excluding pattern 0 from the trained action space.

Production workflow:

- Train skip_regular actor: `scripts/figures/papers/mehregan/5a/plot.py --train`
- Checkpoint: `artifacts/figures/papers/mehregan/4a/checkpoint_skip_regular_02s.pt`
- Fig 4a panel script stays on the **full 41-pattern** space for the training curve; Fig 5a/6a eval use skip_regular checkpoints.

Do not overwrite locked mid-fade full-alphabet assets when experimenting with skip_regular.

## Entropy coefficient (policy-collapse tuning)

`--entropy-coeff` on the panel script (default **0.01**). Raise slightly (e.g. `0.02`) as a paper-silent anti-collapse knob when exploration collapses too fast; `series_v4_ent002` also passes mid-fade gates.
