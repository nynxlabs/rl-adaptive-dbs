## Paper target

Same **45 Hz** DDPG run as Fig 4a, shown as **9 episodes** (indices 0–8):

- **Reward** rises from roughly **−80** toward **0** (still slightly negative late).
- **Episode-mean PSD($×10^3$)** falls ~0.50 → ~0.37.

Fig 4b PSD **is** Fig 4a's per-step β averaged over each 30-step episode. Matching this panel's late floor pulls 4a's absolute level and removes the episode-5 cliff; it does **not** by itself fix 4a's within-episode wiggles.

## Digitization revisit (2026-08-13)

Locked 4b (from `series_v4.json`) passed *ratio* gates while sitting on the wrong floor: late PSD ~0.30 (below $β_t=0.35$) and late reward ~+14. Required gates:

- `ep0_beta_near_paper` — **phase 1:** episode 0 PSD within 10% of digitized ~0.50
- `late_beta_above_threshold` — late episode-mean PSD ≥ 0.35
- `late_beta_near_paper` — late PSD within 15% of digitized ~0.37
- `late_reward_near_zero` — late mean reward in $(-10, 2]$

v31/v33 (init_bias 0, jitter 0.5, τ **3→1.4**) **passed ep0** (PSD 0.499 vs 0.498; reward −76 vs paper ~−80) and still failed the late floor (episode 5→ pattern 0; late PSD ~0.29 / reward +16). **Phase 2** (τ_end **1.6**, v33/v34) only delayed the cliff; late still ~0.29 / +16 with 100% pattern 0 on episodes 6–9. **Phase 3** keeps those knobs and raises `--entropy-coeff 0.05`. Tracker **Status** follows `summary.gates_pass`, not the legacy automation reward-shape gate.

Reward *magnitude* (paper ~−80 vs our ~−30) stays a documented scale mismatch — do not retune Eq. (8) just to stretch the y-axis.
