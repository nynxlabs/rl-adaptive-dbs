# Mehregan Fig 4a — training beta power vs step

Panel spec and **experimental tuning notes** for Mehregan et al. Fig 4a (§IV.A.1). Ship status, replication image, and run commands remain in [docs/controllers/ddpg/README.md](../README.md) § Fig 4a.

**Entry point:** `scripts/figures/papers/mehregan/4a/plot.py`

## Paper target

Per-step GPi beta-band power during DDPG training on the **45 Hz** pattern alphabet: **300** environment steps (10 episodes × 30 steps). Y-axis **PSD(x10³)** = raw $P_\beta / 1000$. Digitized paper: early (steps ≤130) mean ~0.49, late (≥150) ~0.38, drop ~0.11; mid [120,150] ~0.445 vs early [0,100] ~0.49 (modest mid fade over ~30 steps, not a vertical cliff).

## Paper-faithful recipe (Oct 2026)

**Shared recipe:** `scripts/figures/papers/mehregan/paper_protocol.py` (train + closed-loop eval), used by `4a/plot.py`, `efficacy_panel.py` (Fig 5a/5b) and `quant_panel.py` (Fig 6a/6b).

**Paper-silent conventions (each chosen from evidence, not from the gate it would fix):**

- **Exploration:** Gaussian noise on the actor logits, σ = 1.0; executed pattern = $\arg\max$ of the noisy logits, stored $a_{\mathrm{logit}}$ = that noisy vector (so the logits critic scores what was played). σ ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 1.0} were trained at seed 0; only 1.0 passes every Fig 4a + 4b gate (0.25 / 0.5 settle on a deeper suppressor; late reward +2.8 / +5.6 vs paper −2.4).
- **Init bias toward the regular pattern = 1.0** (Oct 17 2026; was 0). §I initializes "the action space … using regular pulses"; 2.0 plays the regular 45 Hz train (a strong suppressor) from episode 0, unlike the paper's untreated-like episode 0, and 1.0 keeps episode 0 at 0.446 (§ Audit).
- **Alphabet:** *(open deviation — Figure 3a specifies a per-pulse slot head, not a menu; see § QAT tie-break → Second pass)* `BurstPatternAlphabet` at both rates — since Oct 17 2026 21 distinct patterns at exactly the mean rate, § Audit (paper: 30 Hz model trained with "all other parameters fixed"). Open-loop eval landscape (`scripts/probes/mehregan_paper_landscape.py`, seed 0, no stim ≈ 486): 30 Hz ±1/3 jitter has 0/40 irregular patterns below no stim (Fig 5b impossible), burst 30/40; at 45 Hz the burst irregular mean ≈ 490 matches the paper's untreated-like episode 0, jitter ≈ 449.
- **Update frequency = one Alg. 1 update every 2nd step** (Oct 17 2026, agreed with the maintainer; `update_every=2`). Alg. 1 line 15 "for update frequency do" sits inside the per-step loop; the literal one-update-every-step reading converges by episode 3, the every-2nd-step reading gives the paper's gradual five-episode fall (§ Candidate readings). Retrained: Fig 4a rep v44, 4b rep v51, 5a rep v26, 5b rep v27, 6a rep v71, 6b rep v49.
- **Plant state carries across the 2 s steps of an episode**; each episode resets to new initial conditions (seed + episode). Episode reward includes the reset segment's reward (Alg. 1 line 7).
- **Seed 0** for training and eval.

**Results (seed 0, σ = 1.0):** Fig 4a passed every mean-based gate; since Oct 18 it fails `early_swing_near_paper` (§ Early-swing gate) (ep0 0.444 vs paper 0.507; late 0.35 vs 0.38). Fig 4b all gates pass (reward −81 → late +0.5 vs paper −81 → −2.4; late PSD 0.349 vs 0.368). The trained policy settles on burst pattern 4; closed-loop Fig 5a: trained 339, periodic 45 Hz 299, no stim 486, 130 Hz 169 — the paper's ordering (trained above periodic, below no stim). Before this rebuild that ordering needed `skip_regular` (removing the regular pattern from the action space); the faithful agent simply does not find the regular pattern in 10 episodes.

**Gate changes (Oct 2026):** `ep0_near_paper` / `ep0_beta_near_paper` tolerance 10% → 15%: episode 0 is exploration over near-zero logits, and seeds 0–9 give 0.43–0.55 (mean 0.47; `scripts/probes/mehregan_ep0_seeds.py`), so a 10% band around the paper's single draw failed 4 of 10 faithful seeds by chance. Fig 4b dropped `late_beta_above_threshold` (restated `late_beta_near_paper` + `late_reward_near_zero` with a 5% margin) and the legacy `automation` gate. `tests/scripts/paper_gates_test.py` feeds each panel's digitized paper curves through its own gates — all pass.

## Early-swing gate (Oct 18 2026)

Every gate above is a window or episode mean, so a run can match the paper's episode means while its per-step trace swings far wider. The shipped menu run does: its early steps jump between suppressing and beta-raising patterns (the 21 patterns span 0.6×–1.58× no stim, 7 of them raise beta), while the paper's early curve wiggles in a narrow band. `early_swing_near_paper` gates that.

**Metric.** Over episodes 0–3 (steps 0–119, before the paper's mid fade), subtract each 30-step episode's own mean and take the standard deviation of what is left — the within-episode, step-to-step spread, with the slow trend removed. The digitized paper curve has 205 points over 300 steps (about one per 1.5 steps), so it cannot resolve every step-to-step jump; sampling our per-step trace at the same x lowers the metric by 0.71–0.93× on our runs. The gate therefore evaluates ours at the paper's digitized x, so both curves share the digitizer's sampling.

**Threshold.** Pass when ours is within **0.5–2×** the paper's (two-sided, the same band as `mid_fade_vs_paper`: a frozen or smoothed trace fails as well as a swinging one). Evidence that the band is neither loose nor unreachable:

| Trace (sampled at the paper's x) | Early swing, eps 0–3 | × paper |
|---|---|---|
| paper (digitized) | 0.0265 | 1.00 |
| paper, per episode (eps 0–9) | 0.015–0.039 | 0.6–1.5 |
| shipped menu run (rep v44) | 0.132 | **4.99** |
| menu seeds / candidates (`s1`, `rd-upd2s2`) | 0.087–0.100 | 3.3–3.8 |
| Fig 3a per-pulse runs (`f3-t1`, `f3-t4`) | 0.020–0.024 | 0.76–0.91 |
| converged late windows (steps 150–299) of our menu runs, seeds 0–4 | 0.014–0.026 | 0.7–1.3 (of the paper's late 0.0195) |

The paper's own episodes vary 0.6–1.5× around its early value, and our converged late windows (the plant's noise floor under one pattern) sit 0.7–1.3× the paper's late value, so 0.5–2× leaves room for plant noise and seed spread; the menu sits 3.3–5× above the paper and fails clearly. For scale: random rate-matched pulse placements on the training env give a per-step spread of about 0.035–0.04 at full resolution (§ Per-pulse candidates), and the paper's sampled 0.0265 corresponds to about 0.03 at full resolution.

`tests/scripts/paper_gates_test.py` passes the digitized paper curve through the gate (ratio ≈ 1) and checks that an added ±0.15 alternating swing and a flat-within-episode trace both fail.

**Result on the shipped recipe:** rep v44 fails `early_swing_near_paper` at 4.99× — the honest outcome of the menu action space. Fig 4a no longer passes every gate.

**Why 5b fails.** The 4-bin histogram pools offsets across pulses: a coherent shift of the train and alternating ± pairs (bursts repeating at 15 Hz, inside the beta band at 30 Hz) look the same to the critic. ## QAT tie-break (Fig 6a / 6b, Oct 2026)

The QAT run uses the same recipe with PyTorch eager QAT (fake-quant stub on the actor input, dequant stub on the logits, `prepare_qat` on Conv1d / Linear). Eq. 5 pushes logits up without bound (the fp32 actor's logits reach $10^3$). Under fake quantization the head output is clamped to the range its moving-average observer has tracked; that range lags the growing logits, and the straight-through gradient is zero above it, so 12–22 logits pile up at the clamp maximum (the seed-0 7-bit actor had raw logits up to 2.0 against a clamp of 1.05 — only 4 distinct output values). QAT therefore never converges in 10 episodes (13–18 distinct actions per late episode vs 1 for fp32) — the paper's "failed to generate a stimulation pattern" mechanism.

**Int8, not 7-bit (Oct 6 2026).** PyTorch's fbgemm QAT default fake-quantizes activations with `reduce_range=True`, i.e. 7 bits (0..127) — an x86 overflow workaround for real int8 kernels. The paper says the forward/backward passes round values "to emulate int8", so `controllers/ddpg/quantization.py` now uses the full 0..255 activation range (weights unchanged: per-channel symmetric int8). This is a fidelity fix chosen from the text; it does not remove the tie mechanism above.

The deployed policy is `argmax`, which returns the **first** tied index, so the QAT trace depends on which low-index pattern the tie resolves to (`scripts/probes/mehregan_qat_seeds.py`, σ = 1.0, eval seed 0; action → late/pre):

| 1 | 0 → 0.59 | 0 → 0.59 | 1 → 1.17 | 2 → 0.68 |

**Observer-freeze probe (Oct 6 2026, rejected).** PyTorch's QAT recipe often stops updating quantization ranges partway through training. Freezing the observers at episode 3 or 6 (`mehregan_qat_seeds.py --freeze-observer-after N`, seeds 0–2, int8) made saturation worse — 19–23 tied logits (vs 12–18), still 19–23 distinct actions in the last episode, and 5/6 45 Hz runs on the regular train. The tie comes from Eq. 5 pushing logits up without bound against a fixed fake-quant range, not from a fixable observer setting; the default (observers on) stays.

### Seed robustness (Oct 12 2026) — QAT is a seed band

**Question.** The shipped QAT trace was one training seed, and the eval action of 10-episode QAT is a tie-break — so does any paper-faithful recipe make QAT paper-like (late/pre ≥ 80% of the digitized paper level) in a clear majority of training seeds?

**Diagnosis (per-episode clamp / tie probe, `scripts/probes/mehregan_qat_seeds.py`, seeds 0–6 at both rates).** In every seed the logit head's moving-average observer range stays at about ±0.7–1.1 for all 10 episodes, while the pre-quant head output keeps running past it (raw max 0.7–1.6). Values above the clamp get zero straight-through gradient, so the actor can only push logits *to* the clamp; the clamp creeps up at the observer's averaging constant (0.01 per forward). Result: 12–20 of the 41 logits share the top int8 code, and σ = 1.0 exploration noise dwarfs the O(1) logit spread, so the behaviour policy stays near random (12–19 distinct actions in episode 10, vs 1 for fp32). The deployed `argmax` resolves the tie to the lowest tied index, the same pattern at every eval step.

**Is the fake-quant eval the deployed model?** Yes. `torch.ao.quantization.convert` with the qnnpack engine gives the same logits within one quantization step at every layer and the same tie and argmax. With fbgemm the converted model drifts by up to 25 steps from conv2 on — fbgemm's u8×s8 int16 accumulation saturates with full-range (not `reduce_range`) activations, a kernel artifact, not a different model. Eval stays fake-quant.

Unless listed, σ = 0.3, γ = 0.99, b = 1.0. The Oct 6–10 candidates (σ ∈ {0.1, 0.3, 1.0}, b 0–1.25, γ 0.99, u1 or u10) show the same picture: `f3h45-u10`'s trained actor has logit std 6.1 and its regular-slot logits pushed to −6, with the head bias still at its init.

**Mechanism.** No run — paper-silent knobs or the deviations — gets more than a quarter of the paper's drop: every 45 Hz training trace stays at 0.47–0.52 for all 300 steps (`artifacts/probes/mehregan_fig3/screen_v1.png`). The actor ends in one of three states, none of them learned:

1. **Runaway logits.** Fig 3b's critic is $\mathrm{Linear}(\mathrm{enc}(s) + \mathrm{ReLU}(W a))$ — piecewise linear and unbounded in the 180,000 logits $a$ — and Eq. 5 moves the actor up its slope. Adam at the stated $5\times10^{-4}$ steps every encoder weight by about the learning rate whatever the gradient size, and `fc1` has fan-in 32,000 over an all-positive state, so its outputs jump by several units per update. On a fixed synthetic batch the logit std goes 0.4 → 6.5 → 101 → 3,800 after 5 / 15 / 30 / 60 updates; Adam ε from $10^{-8}$ to $10^{-3}$ does not change it (encoder gradients are $10^{-2}$–$4\times10^{-2}$, far above ε).
2. **Arbitrary fixed pattern.** When the runaway levels off (logit std 0.3–700), the regular-pulse init is gone after the first episode of updates. The noise-free actor plays one state-independent pattern set by the random head weights times the grown features (late/pre 0.95–1.09, the random-placement band), and σ ≤ 1 exploration no longer changes what is played.
3. **Stuck at the init.** With 3 updates per step and γ ≥ 0.5 the logits stop changing after episode 3 (the encoder is still active: 25–48 of 256 `fc1` units on), so the trained actor is the init — the periodic train, 0.59, which Fig 5a's `trained_above_periodic` rules out.

Annealing σ onto a strong regular init does not produce the paper's curve either: the init is erased before the noise comes down. Even if it survived, that path would only replay the periodic train — below the paper's late 0.38 at 45 Hz, and at 30 Hz it raises beta, which Fig 5b's trained-below-periodic claim forbids. Underneath is credit assignment: 300 transitions cannot tell the critic which of 180,000 logit directions matter, so a bounded critic input (softmax) or a 10–100× smaller actor step only slows the drift to an arbitrary pattern.

**Conclusion.** On the Fig 3 head, Fig 4a cannot pass with the paper's stated mechanism (raw logits into the critic, Eq. 5, Adam at $5\times10^{-4}$, 10 × 30 steps): every paper-silent knob tried leaves the training trace flat, and the deviations tested do not fix it either. The branch stays parked and the 41-pattern menu stays the shipped recipe.

The catch is the fp32 model: it is the same non-learning actor (an arbitrary fixed pattern, 87–90 distinct slots per step), so its weak suppression and the failing Fig 4a remain.
